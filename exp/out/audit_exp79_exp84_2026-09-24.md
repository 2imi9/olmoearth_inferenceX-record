# Audit of exp79 (gate G, P1-P5) and exp84 (P1-P4) final grading

Auditor: independent second route, 2026-09-24. Repository at b2f687f with the working-tree changes listed by
`git status` (exp79_summary.json and exp84_summary.json modified; seven exp79_seeds exports untracked). No file in
the repository was edited. Scripts live in the scratchpad next to this report (`audit_lib.py`, `audit79*.py`,
`audit84*.py`, `audit_p45_compute.py`, `audit_p45_grade.py`, each with a `.log`). They import nothing from `exp/` or `oe_inferencex/`: JSON is read with `json`, the sign test is
exact binomial via `math.comb` in `fractions.Fraction`, Spearman is mid-rank Pearson written with explicit loops,
Holm is written from its definition, and the oracle AURC uses the harmonic-sum closed form
(1/n)[k - (n-k)(H_n - H_{n-k})], not the package's vectorised mean.

Inputs read: docs/plan/seed_floors.md (all amendments, including the 23 September P5 restatement),
docs/plan/where_the_lead_holds.md (with its addendum), exp/exp79_seed_floors.py and
exp/exp84_where_the_lead_holds.py (layout only), exp/exp74_suite_encoders.py `encoder_verdict` (the bar's
tie handling), scripts/headroom_by_encoder.py (the headroom statistic's definition).

### P1: AGREE (FAILS, on the AUROC clause only)
Failing encoder-seeds, 14 of 150, identical to the script's list: galileo_nano all ten seeds (0.322 to 0.387),
clay_large seeds 7 (0.368) and 9 (0.395), galileo_tiny seeds 4 (0.385) and 7 (0.394). The lead clause holds on all
150 (max rho(acc, lead) = -0.1857, satlas_base). Every one of the 150 rho(acc, AUROC) values is positive (min 0.322)
and every rho(acc, lead) is negative.

Adversarial read. Not a harness artifact: no NaN, no ties that matter, the encoder carries its full recorded task
set (21 for galileo_nano). It is not a single outlier task either: leave-one-task-out on galileo_nano seed 0 spans
0.215 to 0.398 and no single removal reaches 0.4; on seed 9 only dropping Togo S1 does (0.401). What drives it is
visible in the seed-0 table: the three CropHarvest Togo tasks and CropHarvest China S2 have mid-to-high accuracy
(0.72 to 0.77) and the lowest AUROCs (0.67 to 0.73). What the ten-of-ten does NOT show: the ten seeds share the same
21 tasks, so they are not ten independent confirmations; the task-sampling standard error of a Spearman on 21 tasks
is about 0.2, so 0.32 against a 0.4 bar is within one SE. That is context, not a softening: the bar was chosen
knowing encoders carry 20-21 tasks, and it failed as written.

Honest sentence: *P1 fails as written. The accuracy-AUROC confound has the predicted sign on all 150 encoder-seeds
(rho 0.32 to 0.78) and the lead's has the predicted sign on all 150 (rho -0.64 to -0.19), but the AUROC correlation
falls below the preregistered 0.4 on 14 of 150: galileo_nano under every seed (0.32 to 0.39), clay_large and
galileo_tiny under two seeds each.*

### P3: AGREE (FAILS, 48 of 150 encoder-seeds, max range 0.1275 satlas_base seed 3)
Failing encoder-seeds by encoder: clay_large 10, croma_large 10, satlas_base 10, copernicusfm 9, croma_base 4,
terramind_large 2, panopticon 1, olmoearth_large 1, galileo_nano 1 (nine encoders at one seed or more; six never).

sensor-group median lead anywhere: 0.0618 (panopticon, S1, seed 6). Identical to the script's failing list.

Adversarial read, three separate questions:
1. *Is the verdict a product of one-task groups?* Partly, and only for one encoder. satlas_base's S1+S2 group is a
   single task (m_so2sat), so its "median" is m_so2sat's lead (0.177 to 0.197), and satlas_base's ten failures and
   the headline max of 0.128 are entirely that task: with groups of at least two tasks satlas_base passes on all ten
   seeds (max 0.053). Dropping one-task groups everywhere leaves 38 of 150 failing (clay_large 10, croma_large 10,
   copernicusfm 9, croma_base 4, terramind_large 2, three encoders at 1), max 0.105 (copernicusfm). So P3 still fails
   without the artifact; the record must not quote 0.128 as a sensor difference without saying it is one task.
2. *Is it seed noise?* No. Averaging each task's lead over the ten seeds first, four encoders still exceed 0.06
   (clay_large 0.072, croma_large 0.073, copernicusfm 0.082, satlas_base 0.102 [one-task group]). *Is it task
   sampling noise in 3-5-task group medians?* Plausibly in part: leave-one-task-out at seed 0 moves an encoder's
   range by up to 0.04-0.09 (satlas_base 0.012 to 0.100, clay_large 0.038 to 0.107, copernicusfm 0.051 to 0.119).
   The bar of 0.06 sits inside that spread, and Base's own disclosed B200 range was already 0.070. But the
   direction is systematic, not noise-shaped: S2 is lowest on 125 of 150.
3. *Is "no sensor is a hole" substantively false?* No. Every sensor group's median lead is at least 0.062 on every
   encoder and seed; pooled medians are S2 0.107, Landsat 0.126, S1+S2 0.132, S1 0.136. What fails is the
   homogeneity bar, not the absence of a hole. The ordering of groups tracks accuracy (pooled median accuracy S2
   0.761, S1+S2 0.735, Landsat 0.695, S1 0.645), which is the same negative accuracy-lead confound P1's second clause
   confirms; a sensor effect net of accuracy was not preregistered and was not tested here.

Honest sentence: *P3 fails as written: the sensor groups' median leads differ by more than 0.06 on 48 of 150
encoder-seeds (38 if one-task groups are excluded; satlas_base's range of up to 0.128 is m_so2sat alone), with S2
lowest on 125 of 150. No sensor is a hole in the sense of the lead vanishing (lowest group median anywhere 0.062),
but the lead is not uniform across sensors to within 0.06, and the preregistered bar is what failed.* The page's
title claim should be written as "the lead is positive in every sensor group; its size varies by more than the
preregistered 0.06", not as "no sensor is a hole" held.
