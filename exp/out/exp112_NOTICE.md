# exp112's per-plot table: source and use restrictions

`exp112_plots.csv` holds, for each plot of exp112's draw of the LCMAP Reference Data Product v1.2 (USGS, public
domain), the class that a logistic regression fitted on Ai2's OlmoEarth v1.3 embeddings predicted there and its top
probability. Those predictions are derived from the embeddings, which Ai2 releases under the OlmoEarth Artifact License
(https://source.coop/olmoearth/olmoearth-v1-3-embeddings/LICENSE.txt).

Source: OlmoEarth v1.3 embeddings, Allen Institute for AI (Ai2), https://allenai.org/papers/olmoearth-v1-3.

As the license requires of any distribution of derivatives, these use restrictions (its Section 2) apply to this table
and to anything derived from it. You will not use it, or encourage, permit or facilitate others to use it, for:

(a) any military and defense-related applications and use cases, including without limitation, for weapons
development, military operations, intelligence gathering, or human surveillance and policing activities;
(b) any extractive activities, operations and use cases involving the removal of raw materials from the earth,
including without limitation, to plan or facilitate the extraction of oil, natural gas and minerals through
activities such as drilling, mining, and deforestation.

The table holds no embedding and no coordinate. The embeddings themselves are not in this repository.
