# Audit: the finite-population Wilson form of 2026-09-23

Own code throughout (`scratchpad/audit.py`, `audit2.py`); the package's `wilson_interval` and `exact_coverage_srs` were called only to compare. Grid: N in {50,100,300,1000}, K in {1,2,3,5,10,20,50}, n in {10,20,30,45,60,100,150,300}, 164 cells; fine sweeps at N = 100, 306, 1000.

## BLOCKING

1. **The amendment's coverage floor is false.** "Under it the exact coverage dips no lower than 0.92 on a grid of (N, K, n) with N ≤ 1000 and K ≥ 2" — on the grid above the new form covers 0.830 at (1000, 3, 60), 0.859 at (1000, 5, 30), 0.871 at (1000, 3, 45) and (300, 2, 20), 0.884 at (1000, 2, 60), 0.897 at (300, 3, 60); the package's own `est.exact_coverage_srs` returns the same 0.8304. The "against 0.50 before" compares a K = 1 cell of the old form with a K ≥ 2 floor of the new; like for like at K ≥ 2 the old form's worst is 0.642 at (100, 2, 60), and at the low-fraction dips the two forms coincide (Wilson's own small-K oscillation). Restate as measured or delete the sentence.
2. **The preregistered expectation for the rerun is already falsified by the four reruns in hand, and the rewritten claim hides it.** "Every other shipped-form cell moves by less than 0.01" — Clay Large: 36 cells moved by more than 0.01 (max 0.073), 11 of them graded shipped-form non-EuroSAT cells, six of those Togo random-design user's-accuracy cells from 1.000 to 0.927–0.976; Copernicus-FM: 24 and 9. The three exemptions the new claim reports ("near-census Togo classes with 12 to 15 errors") are cells the new form created: Clay Togo S2+S1 class 0 covered 1.000 under the old form and 0.927 under the new (exact 0.9244); the record presents them as pre-existing discreteness. The sentence also names "a large fraction of the map count" as the only place the correction matters, which is exactly Togo (300 of 306).
3. **CHANGELOG says "exp81's per-class counts are restated in its section"; they are not.** `docs/results/comparisons.md:2504-2506` still reads "Six further EuroSAT cells sit at 0.63–0.90 by Wilson's discreteness ... and match the exact achievable coverage", `docs/plan/per_class_assessment.md:116-120` still says the same, and `per-class-intervals-wald-fails-wilson-nearly-holds` still pins six discreteness cells — all three contradicted by the fourth amendment in the same files. Until Base's rerun lands, the CHANGELOG line must say "will be restated" or the commit ships a section that contradicts itself.

## MUST STATE

4. **"The Wald counts do not move (Wald has no such term)" is the wrong reason.** `estimate_per_class` computes the random-design user's accuracy with `wilson_interval(k, n_c, N_map[c])` regardless of `interval`, so 55 / 45 / 54 / 48 of the 323 / 274 / 323 / 276 "Wald"-graded cells are the corrected Wilson, and 54 (Clay) and 40 (Copernicus) Wald-cell UA coverages moved between the old and new artifacts, identically to the Wilson cells. The counts 39 and 62 held only because none of the moved cells crossed 0.93 (0 of the Wald failures are random-design UA cells). The record's "whenever a class shows no sampled error the interval is a point at 1" does not describe those cells.
5. **"The two forms agree there to 1e-4" (segmentation cells left as run) is off by 10×.** The k = 0 shift is ≈ z²/N_c: 2.9e-3 at (n = 30, N = 1000), 1.5e-3 at (30, 2000), 7.1e-4 at (100, 5000), 1.65e-4 only at N = 22,598; 1e-4 needs N_c ≳ 38,000. Containment flips only on knife-edge cells, but the stated tolerance is wrong for map classes of a few thousand windows.
6. **"No recorded number moves" holds for every coverage digit and for the cited precision, with one caveat to say.** Exact coverage under the two forms is identical (shift 0.00) at all 42 recorded overall-rate cells (exp78's 7, exp79's 35; containment of θ is the same for every k in 0..300 at N ≥ 12,800), so every SRS, naive and cluster coverage would regenerate byte for byte. Say "not regenerated; no cited digit moves".
7. **P2's third task on Clay and Copernicus is a knife edge the new form produced.** nandi_sentinel1's median ratio went 0.9824 → 1.0011 (Clay) and 0.9864 → 1.0004 (Copernicus); the random-design UA half-width is narrower under the new form on every cell (new/old ratio min 0.445, median 0.989, never above 1) while the confidence design's UA at p = 1 falls back to Wilson at the labelled count with no finite-population correction (`_wilson_eff` → `wilson_interval(n, n)`): at Togo sizes (N_c 150, n_c 147, no error) the random bound is [0, 0.0005] and the confidence fallback [0, 0.0255], 48× wider. The verdict (P2 fails) stands; "3 of 14" measures that asymmetry.
8. **The `exact_ua_coverage` exemption is a tautology, not an exemption.** A deterministic interval's Monte Carlo coverage always equals its exact coverage up to MC error, so "MC within tol of exact" can fail only on MC noise: it exempted the old form's defect (0.70 at (100, 1, 30)) and now exempts the score form's near-census under-coverage (0.9244 / 0.9255 / 0.9254 on the three Togo cells, K_c 12–15, n_c = N_c − 6 .. N_c). Discreteness is real (a class shows 0..min(K_c, n_c) errors) but it can only push an exact interval's coverage above nominal: the exact tail inversion covers 0.989–0.991 on those three cells (plain Wilson without fpc 1.000); what fails there is the normal approximation with 3–6 unlabelled windows per class, which the record's near-census warning already names. Either record such cells as the interval's failures with that mechanism, or exempt only where no interval reaches the bar (never, on this grid).
9. **The new form is not uniformly better than the old; it is calibrated where the old was wide.** Grid means: new 0.949, old 0.920, plain Wilson 0.968, exact 0.990; cells below 0.93: new 40, old 56, plain 21, exact 0. New falls below old by more than 0.02 on 15 cells, all mid-range θ at sampling fraction ≥ 0.3: (100, 10, 60) 0.961 → 0.916, (1000, 20, 300) 0.947 → 0.920, (1000, 10, 300) 0.954 → 0.926, (100, 20, 45) 0.950 → 0.923, (100, 50, 10) 0.984 → 0.908; it rises by more than 0.02 on 35 cells, all K ≤ 2 (e.g. (300, 1, 150) 0.50 → 1.00). Worst cells: old 0.500 at (300, 1, 150); new 0.800 at (100, 1, 20), (50, 1, 10), (300, 1, 60) — the same 0.800 under old and plain Wilson.
10. **Q2 verdict.** The new form is a defensible score interval for a finite population — the algebra is exact, its average coverage is nominal (0.949) and its dips are Wilson's own small-K oscillation plus 0.90–0.93 at near-census — but it is not a guaranteed-95% interval, and the per-class record has now twice written an exemption for its dips. For the per-class path (N_c ≤ 1000, fractions 0.3–0.98, a 0.93 per-cell bar) an exact hypergeometric tail inversion never falls below 0.951 on the grid at a width cost of 4–8% (0.097 → 0.105 at (100, 1, 30); 0.0568 → 0.0590 at (1000, 100, 300)) and removes the exemption; for the overall rate (N ≥ 12,800) the two are indistinguishable and the score form can stay.
